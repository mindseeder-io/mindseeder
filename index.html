How do I turn a ChatGPT conversation into notes?

Open Plant from the menu, select Copy request for AI and paste it into your ChatGPT conversation. Paste the reply back into Plant, check it and plant it.

How can I see a ChatGPT or Claude chat as notes together?

Plant the note bundle, then walk around its trees in the 3D garden or read them side by side as cards in Office view.

Where are my notes saved?

In this browser, no account needed. Clearing browser data deletes them, so keep a copy with Download backup file in the menu and restore it with New garden from backup.

What can I export from a garden?

A harvest document or all notes as Markdown, all notes as JSON, and a backup file. PDF works too but can lose some symbols; Markdown keeps the text exact.

How is a garden different from Obsidian or Notion?

They are better for writing and organising pages. mindseeder is for stacking material, comparing sources and choosing a conclusion, saved as a .md file.
